Oyster Card Wallet Frame

Problem Statement

For context, in London, there are these travel cards called Oyster Cards. They are RFID-enabled and once they are 'topped-up' with money, you're able to ride on TfL services (buses, trains, tubes, etc.) using them. If I'm not mistaken, the first time you purchased an Oyster Card, you used to also be given a PVC Oyster Card Holder for free.

Either way, these holders/wallets are basically just sheets of PVC, that you folded, down a crease in the middle, to close. Over time, as the wallet was folded and unfolded, this crease started to form cracks, and eventually, the wallet would be thrown away or held together with tape...

I definitely think that this is a problem specific to people like me, that store more than just their Oyster Card in that holder, e.g. loyalty cards. And often need to access those other cards, leading to increased wear on the 'hinge'. My main problem was that I'd bought a card holder with a nice design (penguins), and didn't want to throw it away, so I want a hinged frame that I can slot my old wallet into.

My Solution

I'm currently on version 9, trying to come up with a good way to keep the wallet shut, that doesn't wear out too much over time, nor emit stray magnetic fields (though, I may end up going with this, for lack of a better solution).
